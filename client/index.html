About FurniCraft

FurniCraft is an official digital operations platform engineered specifically for custom furniture workshops, carpentry contractors, and on-site fit-out supervisors. FurniCraft helps contractors manage workers, projects, attendance, wages, payroll, payments, and business operations.

Core Platform Capabilities

Furniture Workforce Management

Maintain centralized craftsman rosters, skill categories, daily wages, and project deployments for furniture teams.

Furniture Contractor Management

Empower Thekedars (contractors) with real-time site supervision, mobile attendance capture, and expense tracking.

Attendance and Payroll Management

Automate daily attendance, overtime calculations, cash advance records, and transparent contractor payroll settlements.
