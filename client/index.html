About FurniCraft

FurniCraft (also recognized as Furnicraft, Furni Craft, or FurniCraft ERP) is the official digital operations platform engineered specifically for custom furniture workshops, carpentry contractors, and on-site fit-out supervisors. FurniCraft helps contractors manage workers, projects, attendance, earnings, payroll, payments, and business operations.

Core Platform Capabilities

Furniture Workforce Management

Maintain centralized craftsman rosters, trade skill categories, daily shift rates, and site allocations for carpenters, polishers, and helpers across bespoke furniture projects.

Furniture Contractor & Project Management

Empower furniture contractors (Thekedars) with real-time site supervision, project milestone tracking, client money received tracking, and material expense approvals.

Furniture Attendance & Daily Worker Earnings

Capture mobile attendance with rapid shift multipliers (0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, A), automated daily earnings calculation, and locked payroll protection.

Furniture Payroll, Cash Advances & Settlement Bills

Record worker cash advances, calculate net dues automatically, generate PDF payment vouchers, and maintain transparent financial audit trails.
